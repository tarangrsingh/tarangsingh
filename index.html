01 — About

About

I work at the boundary between magnonics and quantum circuits: coupling spin waves to microwave photons in resonators I design and fabricate myself, then extending that hands-on RF background into superconducting-qubit readout hardware. The direction I want to take into a PhD is circuit elements that borrow spiking, neuron-like dynamics for computation without giving up quantum behavior — hardware that computes more like a brain and still runs on Hilbert space.

02 — Research

Research

Two projects, one thread: microwave circuits that couple to something quantum.

MSc Thesis · Completed

Cavity Magnonics Thesis

An original elliptical spiral resonator design — the Archimedean Spiral Double Split-Ring Resonator (A-SDSRR) — built to couple magnons to microwave photons.

g/2π ≈ 65–70 MHz
coupling peaks near 3 spiral turns

Boards fabricated and measured across five turn counts (N = 1, 3, 5, 7, 9). The work combines simulation in CST Microwave Studio, FR4 fabrication, and characterization using a Rohde & Schwarz ZNLE18 vector network analyzer.

CST PCB fabrication VNA S21 Coupled oscillators
In progress

Superconducting Qubit Readout

Reproducing a multiplexed readout architecture for superconducting qubits, beginning with the physics and geometry engine and the core multiconductor transmission-line coupler.

The longer-term goal is to understand and build circuit elements capable of bringing neuron-like, spiking dynamics into quantum hardware.
