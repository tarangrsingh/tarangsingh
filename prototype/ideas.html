Understanding God

God, and every deed he does to us, reaches us as a mixture of deism and absurdism. He hides himself by making things feel absurd enough, but they are not: everything is intertwined, and it takes conscience and a third-person view to see it. Religion is a cage, and it does the opposite of what it claims — it pulls you away from thinking about god with your own mind. God has to be understood and thought about, not believed and worshipped.
