Paper 1

A lower bound on the irrationality measure of π

The paper bounds how well π can be approximated by rationals: for the set of numbers it considers, any approximation satisfying |π − p/q| ≥ 1/qn cannot have n = 2. In other words the error exponent is strictly larger than the one a generic irrational admits.
